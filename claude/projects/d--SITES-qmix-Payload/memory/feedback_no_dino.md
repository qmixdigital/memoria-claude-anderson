Never mention Dino in any content - they are a direct competitor.

**Why:** Dino only does mass press releases, publishing the same article across multiple news portals with nofollow links. This is bad for SEO. QMix's actual service is guest posts and editorial articles in news portals and blogs, which generate dofollow backlinks.

**How to apply:** When writing about link building strategies for the Brazilian market, always reference guest posts in news portals and blogs (Exame, InfoMoney, TechTudo, etc.) instead of press release platforms. Never recommend mass press release distribution services.
